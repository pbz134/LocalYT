+++LocalYT Revamped - Changelog+++

v3.70
- Added settings.html; The new settings page allows you to
  - Fully customize your personal video recommendation algorithm
  - Edit and delete tag preferences, or fully reset your algorithm
  - Edit your account name & password, or delete your account

v3.65
- Fixed a bug that made the video UI disappear when entering fullscreen with the F key
- Fixed and enhanced video recommendation algorithm
  - Video recommendation AND video caching now works at the same time, allowing for flawless use of huge databases
- Fixed liked_videos.html; Liked videos now display properly again
- Channels on index.html can now be opened with a middle click
- Video titles are now being displayed inside the video container
- Video recommendations are now even more accurate
  - When watching a video of a certain channel, at least two videos of that channel will be recommended on the side bar
  - The mix is ~70% videos with similar tags and ~30% videos suiting your general preferences
- Added Discord embeddings to video.html
  - When sharing a LocalYT video link on Discord, an embedding with a video player and the video title gets shown
  - Note that your LocalYT instance needs to be publicly accessible for this, e.g. by using a Cloudflare tunnel
- Improved playlist video sorting
  - Numbered videos (e.g. game playthroughs) will now be sorted properly
- Fixed an issue where recommendations for new accounts were empty

v3.60
- Added support for playlists on video.html
  - Opening a channel playlist now displays all items on the right side above the recommendations
- Added full .mp3 support
  - Audio file covers will now be displayed permanently in the video player
  - Makes use of a dynamic script loader to use a modified Fluid Player script
- Optimized channel.html with more efficient video loading on large channels
- Included thumbnails in MovePlaylistMetadata.py
- Fixed Lazy Load trigger on channel.html
- Made videos on channel.html mouse-wheel clickable
- playlist.html now shows the channel information
- Added a new script to fix a double-space inconsistency between video files and metadata files
- Fixed line break issue on search.html
- Renamed "SHARE" -> "Share" on video.html
- Reduced margin of video suggestion titles on video.html
- Fixed createStats.py to not respect playlists
- Made channel banner on playlist.html responsive
- Added a progress bar to MovePlaylistMetadata.py
- Changed generateThumbnails.py to output less unnecessary lines to the terminal
- Added a dynamic wait time to #Launch-Server.bat
  - Localhost now only opens if the server is ready instead of waiting a fixed 7 seconds

v3.50
- Added caching to make loading of huge file databases possible, even on HDDs (tested successfully with 9TB of video/audio files)
- Fixed character encoding in algorithm setup scripts
- Added placeholder lengths of 3:00 for video or audio files where createvideolengths.py can't determine their length
- Added tagging support for .mp3 and .mkv files
- Conditional tagging for ASMR and audio files without an LLM
  - For music and ASMR, automatic tags will now be applied, saving tons of time and compute
- Fixed .mp3 compatibility for createvideolengths.py
- Fixed view count and upload date fetching on search.html and channel.html
- Fixed display of thumbnails that are being shown before the initial video play if the video is in a playlist

- Fixed third tag for videos that are in channel playlists on analyze.py

- Aligned view counts on search.html
- Slightly adjusted spacing for recommended videos on video.html
- Add a grid of video suggestions (4x3) after the current video is over
